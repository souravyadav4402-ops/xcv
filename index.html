Our Story

Born in NSP, loved by Delhi.

Modi Mania Cafe started as a small corner at Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura — and grew into Delhi's go-to 24×7 vegetarian hangout. From college students to working professionals, everyone finds their comfort food here.

We believe in simple things done brilliantly — fresh ingredients, bold flavours, and a welcoming atmosphere no matter what time you walk in. That's been our recipe since day one.
