Our Story

From NSP's Streets To Your Heart

Modi Mania Cafe was born from a simple belief: Delhi deserves a place where great vegetarian food is available any time — day or night, weekday or holiday. Nestled in the heart of Netaji Subhash Place, we've been serving up fresh, flavour-packed dishes that keep our guests coming back, again and again.

From a humble beginning with a handful of recipes, we've grown into NSP's most-loved 24×7 destination with over 60 menu items crafted with care. Every ingredient is sourced fresh, every dish is made to order, and every guest is treated like family.
